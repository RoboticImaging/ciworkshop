01 Submissions

Call for Posters.

The 1st Australian Computational Imaging Workshop will bring together the imaging community from fields including robotics, astronomy, medical imaging, signal processing, sensing and beyond.

We invite researchers and practitioners to present their work during the workshop poster sessions. These sessions aim to foster new connections and collaborations across the broader imaging community.

We welcome recently published and yet-to-be-published work. The poster sessions are non-archival.

Submission

Authors wishing to showcase their work are invited to submit a one-page extended abstract describing the core motivation, methods and findings, with relevant figures. References are excluded from the one-page limit.

Double-blind submission requirements: Do not include author names, affiliations, acknowledgements, or other identifying information in the PDF. Enter the author names and affiliations in the OpenReview submission form instead. Where possible, cite prior work in the third person and check the document for identifying metadata.

Extended abstracts must use the workshop template and be submitted in PDF format.

Review

Each extended abstract will undergo double-blind review by the Program Committee and will be assessed for relevance and quality. Reviewer identities will be withheld from authors, and author identities will be withheld from reviewers. Accepted contributions will be invited for poster presentation during the workshop.
