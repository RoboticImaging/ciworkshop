Posters

The first Australian Computational Imaging Workshop will bring together the imaging community from across domains including robotics, astronomy, medical imaging, signal processing, sensing and beyond. We invite you to present your work during the workshop poster sessions to foster new connections and collaborations across the broader imaging community. The poster sessions provide an opportunity to showcase recently published or yet-to-be-published work in a non-archival format to the community.

We seek submissions focussing on computational imaging methods, technologies and hardware in, but not limited to, the following areas:

  • Robotics
  • Astronomy and space technologies
  • Microscopy
  • Medical imaging
  • Signal processing and inverse problems
  • Neuromorphic vision and sensing
  • Meta-optics and diffractive optics
  • Flow visualisation
  • Photography and consumer devices
  • Remote sensing and geophysics
  • Material and industrial imaging
  • Computer graphics and interactive technologies

Accepted abstracts will be made available to workshop attendees before the workshop begins, but will not form archival proceedings.

Submission

Authors wishing to showcase their work are invited to submit a 1-page extended abstract encompassing the core motivation, methods and findings, with relevant figures. References are not included in the 1-page limit.

Please include all author names and affiliations in the extended abstract. Review will be single-blind, so authors should not anonymise the PDF.

Authors are invited to use this Overleaf template. We also provide a Google Docs template. Abstracts are to be submitted in PDF format to the OpenReview site.

During submission, you will be asked to select which domain of computational imaging is most relevant to your work to support reviewer matching.

Submissions open Monday 30 November 2026 and are due Sunday 10 January 2027, 11:59pm AEDT. We anticipate notifying authors of outcomes on or before Monday 18 January 2027.

Review

Each abstract submission will undergo single-blind adjudication through OpenReview by the program committee and be judged on relevance, quality and impact to the workshop.

Poster Format

Posters will be displayed digitally on 16:9 format screens in PORTRAIT orientation. Your poster presentation details will be provided in your acceptance notification.

We encourage you to use the CI Workshop Logo on your poster. There is no set poster template.

Best Student Poster Award

During the poster sessions, members of the awards committee will judge student submissions for the Best Student Poster Award (A$300). When submitting your abstract, you will be asked whether the lead author of the work is a current student to be considered for the award.

Travel Support

There may be some travel support available to students and ECRs who are lead authors of accepted abstracts. During submission, you will be asked whether you would like to be considered for travel support to attend the workshop, and if so:

  • what you are most looking forward to getting out of the workshop by participating,
  • what additional funding is available to support your attendance from your university, and
  • whether you would be able to attend the workshop without receiving travel support.

Please note that due to limited funds, we cannot guarantee funding to everyone. We will do our best to support the broadest attendance. If your institution or company is in a position to sponsor additional places, please contact the Sponsorship chair via hello@ciworkshop.au.

Breakout Sessions

We invite you to propose a special-topic breakout session or focused discussion group. The aim is to share knowledge, upskill members, bridge disciplines, or focus attention on a specific problem space in the Australian computational imaging community.

Up to 3 hours are set aside for community-initiated sessions, and the organising committee will select up to 6 topics from the submitted proposals to convene breakout sessions.

Submission

Proposals should outline the topic, format, organisers and affiliations, and any requirements for the session. Please let us know in the proposal what you need to make your session a success, and we will do our best to accommodate.

Proposals are due Friday 8 January 2027, 11:59pm AEDT. We expect to notify organisers of the outcome on or before Monday 18 January 2027.

Please submit your breakout session proposals via this Google Form.

Selection

We encourage proposals that foster collaboration across disciplines, including discussions of transferable methods. To encourage as much involvement in the community as possible, preference will be given to proposals organised by cross-institution teams and to those that include hands-on interaction by workshop participants.
